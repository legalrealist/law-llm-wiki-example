## Rule
District courts have broad discretion to stay or dismiss a declaratory judgment action in favor of parallel state court proceedings; the Brillhart standard (not the Colorado River standard) applies.

## Significance
Confirmed that the deferential Brillhart standard governs abstention in declaratory judgment cases.
