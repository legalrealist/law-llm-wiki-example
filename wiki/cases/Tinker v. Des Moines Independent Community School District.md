## Rule
Students do not "shed their constitutional rights at the schoolhouse gate"; schools may restrict student speech only if it materially and substantially interferes with school discipline or the rights of others.

## Significance
Landmark student speech case; established the "substantial disruption" test for school restrictions on student expression.
