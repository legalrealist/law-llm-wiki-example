## Rule
An attorney who has represented a client in a matter may not subsequently represent a party adverse to that client in the same or a substantially related matter; if the prior representation is substantially related, it is irrebuttably presumed that confidential information was disclosed.

## Significance
Established the "substantial relationship" test for former client conflicts under what is now MR 1.9; the irrebuttable presumption of disclosure prevents attorneys from arguing they never actually received relevant confidences.
