## Rule
"Use" of a firearm under 18 U.S.C. § 924(c) requires active employment of the weapon, not mere proximity to or possession of it during a drug offense.

## Significance
Narrowed the scope of § 924(c) by rejecting "storage" or "availability" as sufficient to constitute "use," prompting Congress to amend the statute to add "in furtherance of" as an alternative basis for liability.
