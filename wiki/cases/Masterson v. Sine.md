# Masterson v. Sine

## Citation
68 Cal. 2d 222 (1968)

## Issue

## Holding

## Rule
Extrinsic evidence of a collateral oral agreement is admissible if the written agreement is not a complete integration; courts must consider whether the parties would naturally have included the collateral term in the writing.

## Significance
Traynor's opinion adopting a flexible approach to the parol evidence rule; influential in Restatement (Second) approach.
