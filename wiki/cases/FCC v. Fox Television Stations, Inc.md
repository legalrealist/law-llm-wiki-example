## Rule
An agency changing its policy need not provide heightened justification beyond what is required for new policy; it must acknowledge the change and provide a reasoned explanation, accounting for reliance interests if they exist.

## Significance
Clarified the arbitrary-and-capricious standard for agency policy changes post-State Farm.
