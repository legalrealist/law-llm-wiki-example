# Mills v. Wyman

## Citation

## Rule
A moral obligation to pay for past services rendered to a third party, without a prior legal obligation, is insufficient consideration to support a subsequent promise to pay.

## Significance
Classic case rejecting moral obligation as a substitute for consideration; contrast with Webb v. McGowin.
