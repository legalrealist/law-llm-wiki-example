# Bullcoming v. New Mexico

## Citation
564 U.S. 647 (2011)

## Facts
Stub — to be completed.

## Issue
Stub — to be completed.

## Holding
Stub — to be completed.

## Rule
A forensic laboratory report is testimonial; the Confrontation Clause requires the analyst who prepared and certified the report to testify — a surrogate analyst who did not perform or observe the test cannot satisfy the Clause.

## Significance
Extended Melendez-Diaz; the specific certifying analyst must testify, not a surrogate colleague.
