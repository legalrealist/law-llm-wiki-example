## Rule
A judge has absolute immunity from § 1983 liability for judicial acts, regardless of how erroneous or unauthorized; immunity is lost only for non-judicial acts or acts taken in complete absence of jurisdiction.

## Significance
Expansively defined judicial immunity — even unauthorized or egregious judicial acts are protected.
