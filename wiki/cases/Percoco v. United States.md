## Rule
The honest services fraud statute, 18 U.S.C. § 1346, does not extend to private individuals unless they owed a fiduciary duty to the public at the time of the alleged fraud.

## Significance
Limited the scope of § 1346 by holding that a private citizen — even one with significant informal influence over government — cannot be convicted of honest services fraud absent a cognizable fiduciary duty to the public, curbing prosecutorial overreach.
