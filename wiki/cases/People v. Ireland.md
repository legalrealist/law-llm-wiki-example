# People v. Ireland

## Citation

## Rule
The felony murder rule does not apply when the predicate felony is an integral part of and included in fact within the homicide itself (assault with a deadly weapon); applying felony murder in such cases would effectively eliminate the requirement of malice for second-degree murder.

## Significance
Established the "merger doctrine" limitation on felony murder in California; assaultive felonies that merge with the homicidal act cannot serve as the predicate felony for felony murder.
