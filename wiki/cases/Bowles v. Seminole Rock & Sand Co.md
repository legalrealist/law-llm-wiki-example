## Citation

## Rule
When an agency's regulation is ambiguous, the agency's interpretation of it is of controlling weight unless plainly erroneous or inconsistent with the regulation.

## Significance
Original source of Seminole Rock/Auer deference; later reaffirmed in Auer v. Robbins and limited in Kisor v. Wilkie.
