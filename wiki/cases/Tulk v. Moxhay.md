## Rule
A restrictive covenant runs with the land in equity if the subsequent purchaser had notice of it; equity will enforce the covenant against one who takes with notice even absent privity of contract.

## Significance
Foundational English case creating the doctrine of equitable servitudes; allows restrictive covenants to bind successors who take with notice.
