## Rule
The Military Selective Service Act's registration requirement applicable only to men did not violate equal protection; Congress acted well within its constitutional authority in requiring only men to register because only men are eligible for combat, and the purpose of registration is to prepare for combat.

## Significance
Applied intermediate scrutiny but deferred to congressional judgment on military matters; exclusion of women from draft registration upheld because it was substantially related to the important interest in preparing for combat.
