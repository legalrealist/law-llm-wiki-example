## Rule
Sentences that fall outside the U.S. Sentencing Guidelines range are reviewed on appeal for abuse of discretion, not under a heightened standard; courts of appeals may not require "extraordinary" circumstances to justify a variance.

## Significance
Firmly established that post-Booker sentencing review uses an abuse-of-discretion standard for all sentences and that district courts have broad discretion to vary from the Guidelines based on individualized § 3553(a) factors.
