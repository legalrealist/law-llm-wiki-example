# Pike v. Bruce Church, Inc.

## Citation
397 U.S. 137 (1970)

## Facts
Arizona required that cantaloupes grown in Arizona be packaged and labeled within the state before being shipped out, in order to associate the Arizona name with high-quality produce. Bruce Church, a large cantaloupe grower, challenged the requirement as an unconstitutional burden on interstate commerce.

## Issue
Whether Arizona's requirement that cantaloupes grown in Arizona be packaged within the state before shipment violated the Dormant Commerce Clause.

## Holding
The Court struck down the Arizona requirement, finding the burden it imposed on interstate commerce was clearly excessive in relation to the putative local benefits.

## Rule
(Pike Balancing Test) A facially neutral law with incidental burdens on interstate commerce is valid unless the burden on commerce is clearly excessive in relation to the putative local benefits.
