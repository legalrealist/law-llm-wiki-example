## Rule
A private political club's all-white pre-primary election that effectively determined the outcome of the Democratic primary constituted state action; the Fifteenth Amendment prohibits racial discrimination in elections regardless of whether the discriminating entity is formally governmental.

## Significance
Extends the white primary cases (Smith v. Allwright) to private clubs whose elections effectively control the outcome of official elections; government cannot permit private discrimination to subvert voting rights.
