## Rule
A restrictive covenant may be enforced even where conditions in the surrounding area have changed, so long as the covenant still confers real and substantial benefit on the dominant estate.

## Significance
Applied the changed conditions doctrine narrowly; covenants survive unless the restricted area itself has changed so fundamentally that enforcement would be inequitable.
