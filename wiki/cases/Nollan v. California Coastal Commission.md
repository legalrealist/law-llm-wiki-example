## Rule
A government condition on a building permit constitutes an unconstitutional taking unless there is an "essential nexus" between the condition imposed and the legitimate government interest advanced by the permit requirement.

## Significance
First major exactions case; established the essential nexus test, refined in Dolan v. City of Tigard.
