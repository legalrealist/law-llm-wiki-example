## Rule
Under the MPC substantial step test, bringing rat poison to school with intent to poison a teacher constitutes a substantial step toward attempted murder; the MPC broadens attempt liability by focusing on what the defendant has done rather than how close they came to completion.

## Significance
Illustrates MPC attempt analysis; the "substantial step" test is more defendant-friendly than the common law "last proximate act" or "dangerous proximity" tests.
