## Rule
A tipper receives a personal benefit sufficient to establish tipping liability under Dirks when the tipper makes a gift of confidential information to a trading relative or friend; no additional proof of tangible benefit is required.

## Significance
Confirmed and extended Dirks; resolved a circuit split by holding that a gift to a trading relative is itself a sufficient personal benefit.
