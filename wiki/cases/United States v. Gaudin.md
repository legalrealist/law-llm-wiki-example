## Rule
Materiality is an element of 18 U.S.C. § 1001 that must be submitted to and decided by the jury, not the judge.

## Significance
Established that the Constitution requires materiality — a mixed question of law and fact — to be resolved by the jury in false statement prosecutions, limiting judicial power to direct verdicts on this element.
