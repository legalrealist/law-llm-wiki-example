## Rule
Qualified immunity analysis must proceed in sequence: (1) first determine whether the facts alleged show a constitutional violation; (2) only then determine whether the right was clearly established (overruled by Pearson v. Callahan).

## Significance
Established mandatory two-step qualified immunity analysis, later made discretionary by Pearson v. Callahan.
