## Rule
Transactions between a parent and its subsidiary are subject to the intrinsic fairness standard only when the parent receives a benefit at the expense of the subsidiary's minority shareholders; otherwise the business judgment rule applies.

## Significance
Defined when parent-subsidiary transactions trigger entire fairness review vs. business judgment rule; key case for controlling shareholder duty of loyalty.
