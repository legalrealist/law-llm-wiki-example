## Rule
California adopted pure comparative negligence, replacing the all-or-nothing contributory negligence rule; a plaintiff's recovery is reduced in proportion to their share of fault, even if greater than defendant's.

## Significance
Leading case adopting pure comparative negligence; California Supreme Court applied it retroactively and prospectively.
