## Rule
An agency's interpretation of a statute contained in an opinion letter (not a formal rule or adjudication) is entitled to Skidmore deference — respect proportional to its power to persuade — not Chevron deference.

## Significance
Clarified that Chevron deference applies only to formal agency interpretations with the force of law; less formal guidance gets only Skidmore respect.
