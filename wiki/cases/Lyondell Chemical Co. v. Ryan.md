## Rule
Directors are not required to show that they took every possible step to maximize value; Revlon does not require directors to act immediately upon learning a sale is possible. Bad faith requires intentional dereliction of duty.

## Significance
Clarified that Revlon duties require reasonable efforts, not perfection; "bad faith" sets a high bar requiring conscious disregard of duty.
