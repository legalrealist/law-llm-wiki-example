## Rule
Where the state has provided an opportunity for full and fair litigation of a Fourth Amendment claim, federal habeas corpus does not provide a vehicle for raising the claim again.

## Significance
Barred Fourth Amendment suppression claims on federal habeas when the state provided a full and fair opportunity to litigate them; limits the exclusionary rule's reach.
