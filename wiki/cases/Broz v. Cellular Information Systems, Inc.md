## Rule
A corporate opportunity need not be formally presented to all entities with which a fiduciary is associated; the relevant question is whether the opportunity properly belongs to one of those entities under the ALI/Delaware multi-factor test.

## Significance
Clarified corporate opportunity doctrine for directors serving multiple companies; applied the four-factor Delaware test for identifying corporate opportunities.
