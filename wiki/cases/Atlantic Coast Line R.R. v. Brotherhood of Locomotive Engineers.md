## Rule
The Anti-Injunction Act absolutely prohibits federal courts from enjoining state court proceedings except in the three statutory exceptions; the Act is not subject to implied exceptions based on equity.

## Significance
Established that the Anti-Injunction Act's exceptions are to be strictly construed; no equitable exception exists.
