## Rule
A defendant cannot prospectively waive the protections of the Speedy Trial Act, 18 U.S.C. § 3161, because the Act's exclusion provisions are for the benefit of both the defendant and the public.

## Significance
Reinforced that Speedy Trial Act protections are not merely personal rights that defendants may waive; courts cannot honor blanket prospective waivers, ensuring judicial oversight of delay even with defendant consent.
