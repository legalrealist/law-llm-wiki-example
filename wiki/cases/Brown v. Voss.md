## Rule
Use of an easement appurtenant to benefit a non-dominant parcel constitutes misuse of the easement, but an injunction is not automatic; courts balance the equities, including actual harm to the servient estate.

## Significance
Washington Supreme Court declined injunction despite easement misuse where no actual harm was shown; illustrates equitable flexibility in easement cases.
