## Rule
In addition to an essential nexus (Nollan), permit conditions must bear "rough proportionality" to the projected impact of the proposed development; the government must make an individualized determination of proportionality.

## Significance
Added the rough proportionality requirement to the Nollan nexus test; together they form the Nollan/Dolan exactions framework.
