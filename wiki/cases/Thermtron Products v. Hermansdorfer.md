## Rule
District courts may not remand removed cases on grounds other than those authorized by statute; remand orders based on unauthorized grounds are reviewable by mandamus.

## Significance
Limited district court discretion to remand; remand orders are generally not reviewable but those based on unauthorized grounds are.
