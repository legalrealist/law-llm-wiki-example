## Rule
The First Amendment does not permit the government to create new categories of unprotected speech based on a categorical balancing of costs and benefits; the historical approach to unprotected categories governs.

## Significance
Rejected government's attempt to create a new unprotected category for depictions of animal cruelty; reinforced the categorical approach to First Amendment analysis.
