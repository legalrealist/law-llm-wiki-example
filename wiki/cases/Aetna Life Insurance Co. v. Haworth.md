## Rule
A declaratory judgment action satisfies the case-or-controversy requirement if the dispute is definite and concrete, touching the legal relations of parties having adverse legal interests.

## Significance
Upheld the constitutionality of the Declaratory Judgment Act and defined the Article III case-or-controversy standard for declaratory relief.
