## Rule
Under the automobile exception, police with probable cause to search a vehicle may do so without a warrant; if the vehicle is not searched at the scene, it may be taken to the station and searched there without a warrant.

## Significance
Extended Carroll's automobile exception; police need not search on the spot — the exception travels with the vehicle to the station.
