## Rule
Mere possession of material nonpublic information does not create a duty to disclose under Rule 10b-5; a duty to disclose arises only from a relationship of trust and confidence (classical theory).

## Significance
Established the classical theory of insider trading liability — duty must derive from a relationship with the issuer or its shareholders.
