# Hardesty v. Smith

## Citation
3 Ind. 39 (1851)

## Facts
Stub — to be completed.

## Issue
Stub — to be completed.

## Holding
Stub — to be completed.

## Rule
A promise to pay for an invention already made and delivered is unenforceable for lack of consideration; past consideration is not good consideration because it was not given in exchange for the promise.

## Significance
Classic illustration of the past consideration rule; reinforces that consideration must be bargained for and given in exchange for the promise — acts already completed before the promise cannot supply consideration.
