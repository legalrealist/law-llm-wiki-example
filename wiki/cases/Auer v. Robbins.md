## Rule
An agency's interpretation of its own ambiguous regulation is controlling unless plainly erroneous or inconsistent with the regulation.

## Significance
Established Auer (Seminole Rock) deference to agency interpretations of their own regulations; doctrine limited by Kisor v. Wilkie (2019).
