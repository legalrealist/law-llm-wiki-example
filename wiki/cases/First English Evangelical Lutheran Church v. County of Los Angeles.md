## Rule
If a regulation constitutes a taking, the government must pay just compensation for the period during which the taking was in effect, even if the regulation is subsequently invalidated or repealed.

## Significance
Established that temporary regulatory takings require compensation; government cannot avoid the Takings Clause by later invalidating the offending regulation.
