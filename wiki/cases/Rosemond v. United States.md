## Rule
To aid and abet a crime requiring use of a firearm, the defendant must (1) take an affirmative act in furtherance of the offense, and (2) have advance knowledge that a confederate will use a gun during the crime.

## Significance
Defined the mens rea required for aiding and abetting § 924(c) firearms offenses; advance knowledge of the firearm is required.
