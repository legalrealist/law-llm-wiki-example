## Rule
Defendants who reconnoitered a bank, obtained masks, and drove to the bank but abandoned the robbery before entering had taken a substantial step toward attempted robbery; preparation sufficiently corroborative of criminal purpose satisfies the MPC substantial step test even if the defendants voluntarily desisted.

## Significance
Key Second Circuit case on the MPC substantial step test for attempt; shows that conduct prior to entry into a target location can constitute an attempt.
