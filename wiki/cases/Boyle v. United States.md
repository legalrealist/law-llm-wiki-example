## Rule
A RICO "enterprise" need not have a formal hierarchy, fixed membership, or ascertainable structure beyond what is necessary to commit the predicate racketeering acts.

## Significance
Broadly defined the RICO enterprise element, making it easier to prosecute loosely organized criminal groups by rejecting the requirement of a formal organizational structure separate from the pattern of racketeering activity.
