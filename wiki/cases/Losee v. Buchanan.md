## Rule
New York rejected the rule of Rylands v. Fletcher; absent negligence, a person is not liable for damages caused by the explosion of a boiler on their property.

## Significance
Classic American rejection of strict liability for natural/non-natural use; illustrates the historical dominance of fault-based liability in US tort law.
