## Rule
A subsequent purchaser who takes title with knowledge of existing regulations is not barred from asserting a takings claim; however, regulations in place at the time of acquisition may still inform the Penn Central analysis.

## Significance
Rejected the "notice rule" that would bar takings claims for regulations predating acquisition; remanded for Penn Central analysis.
