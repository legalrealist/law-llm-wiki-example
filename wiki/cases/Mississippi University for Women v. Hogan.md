# Mississippi University for Women v. Hogan

## Citation

## Rule
Sex-based classifications must serve important governmental objectives and must be substantially related to achievement of those objectives; a state nursing school's single-sex admission policy violated equal protection because the state failed to show the policy was substantially related to an important interest (the policy actually perpetuated gender stereotypes).

## Significance
Applied intermediate scrutiny to strike down sex-based exclusion; reinforced that sex classifications cannot rest on archaic or stereotyped views of gender roles even when the classification might seem to benefit women.
