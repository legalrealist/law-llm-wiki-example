## Rule
The government must obtain a warrant supported by probable cause before accessing historical cell-site location information (CSLI), even though the records are held by a third-party carrier.

## Significance
Significant Fourth Amendment decision carving out an exception to the third-party doctrine for comprehensive digital location records, recognizing that the pervasive and detailed nature of CSLI warrants heightened privacy protection.
