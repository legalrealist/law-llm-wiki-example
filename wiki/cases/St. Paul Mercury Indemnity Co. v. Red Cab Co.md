## Rule
Amount in controversy is assessed at the time of filing; subsequent events that reduce the recovery below the jurisdictional amount do not divest jurisdiction.

## Significance
Established the "legal certainty" standard — dismissal only appropriate if it appears to a legal certainty that the claim is really for less than the jurisdictional amount.
