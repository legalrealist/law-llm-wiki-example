## Rule
The Takings Clause prevents the government from forcing some people alone to bear public burdens which, in all fairness and justice, should be borne by the public as a whole.

## Significance
Articulated the distributional rationale for the Takings Clause; Justice Black's formulation quoted in virtually every regulatory takings case.
