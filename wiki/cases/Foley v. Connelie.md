## Rule
States may restrict certain governmental positions — particularly those involving policymaking or the exercise of important governmental functions (the "political function" exception) — to citizens, even though alienage is normally subject to strict scrutiny; a state may bar aliens from serving as state troopers.

## Significance
Establishes the "political function" exception to strict scrutiny for alienage classifications; police officers exercise core governmental authority and states may limit such positions to citizens.
