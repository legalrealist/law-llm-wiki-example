## Rule
A single lot owner may enforce a restrictive covenant against a developer even when all other lot owners have consented to modification; the covenant is a property right that courts will enforce at the request of any benefited party.

## Significance
One holdout successfully enforced a residential-use covenant against a hospital; demonstrates the strong property-right character of restrictive covenants.
