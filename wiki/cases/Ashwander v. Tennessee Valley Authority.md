# Ashwander v. Tennessee Valley Authority

## Citation
297 U.S. 288 (1936)

## Facts
Preferred stockholders of a power company challenged the validity of a contract between the company and the Tennessee Valley Authority for the purchase of electric power, arguing the TVA's construction of Wilson Dam and sale of surplus power were unconstitutional.

## Issue
Whether the TVA's power contracts were constitutional, and on what grounds courts should resolve constitutional questions.

## Holding
The Court upheld the contracts as a valid exercise of federal power; Justice Brandeis's famous concurrence articulated seven rules for avoiding constitutional adjudication.

## Rule
Courts should decide cases on the narrowest grounds available, avoiding constitutional questions when possible (the "Ashwander rules" or constitutional avoidance canon).
