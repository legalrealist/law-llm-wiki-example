## Rule
A liquidated damages clause is enforceable if: (1) damages were difficult to estimate at the time of contracting, and (2) the amount stipulated was a reasonable forecast of compensatory damages, not a penalty; the clause is judged at the time of contract formation, not after breach.

## Significance
Classic formulation of the two-part test for enforcing liquidated damages clauses; emphasizes prospective analysis at contracting time rather than comparing the clause amount to actual damages suffered.
