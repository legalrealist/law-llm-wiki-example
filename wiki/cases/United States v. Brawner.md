## Rule
Adopted the ALI Model Penal Code test for insanity: a defendant is not responsible if, as a result of mental disease or defect, they lacked substantial capacity either to appreciate the criminality of their conduct or to conform their conduct to the requirements of law.

## Significance
D.C. Circuit adopted the ALI/MPC insanity test, replacing the M'Naghten test; influential in federal and state courts before the Insanity Defense Reform Act of 1984.
