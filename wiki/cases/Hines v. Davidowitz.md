# Hines v. Davidowitz

## Citation
312 U.S. 52 (1941)

## Facts
Pennsylvania enacted its own alien registration law requiring aliens to register with the state and carry identification cards, shortly after Congress passed the federal Alien Registration Act establishing a comprehensive national alien registration system. Davidowitz challenged the state law as preempted.

## Issue
Whether Pennsylvania's alien registration law is preempted by the federal Alien Registration Act.

## Holding
The Court struck down Pennsylvania's alien registration law as preempted by the comprehensive federal statute governing alien registration.

## Rule
When Congress enacts a comprehensive regulatory scheme in a field touching on foreign affairs, state laws that stand as obstacles to the accomplishment of federal objectives are preempted (obstacle preemption).
