## Rule
A defendant is competent to stand trial if they have sufficient present ability to consult with their lawyer with a reasonable degree of rational understanding and a rational as well as factual understanding of the proceedings against them.

## Significance
Established the constitutional standard for competency to stand trial; applies at all critical stages of criminal proceedings.
