## Rule
The "knowingly or intentionally" mens rea in 21 U.S.C. § 841 requires the government to disprove a defendant's good faith reliance on authorization to prescribe controlled substances.

## Significance
Raised the government's burden in prosecuting physicians under § 841 by requiring disproof of good-faith reliance on authorization, protecting doctors who act in good faith within the scope of their DEA registration.
