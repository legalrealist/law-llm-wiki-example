## Rule
A condition restricting use of property to a specific organization is valid as a use restriction even if it incidentally restrains alienation; courts distinguish use conditions from direct restraints on alienation.

## Significance
Illustrates tension between valid use restrictions and void restraints on alienation; conditions subsequent on property use upheld.
