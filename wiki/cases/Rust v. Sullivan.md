## Rule
The government may condition a subsidy on the recipient not engaging in certain speech (here, abortion counseling); when the government subsidizes only part of a program, it need not fund the disfavored activity.

## Significance
Upheld the "gag rule" on Title X family planning clinics; key government-speech/unconstitutional-conditions case.
