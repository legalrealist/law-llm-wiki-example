# Skidmore v. Swift & Co.

## Citation

## Rule
Agency interpretations in manuals and opinion letters, while not controlling, are entitled to respect proportional to their power to persuade — based on thoroughness, validity of reasoning, and consistency with prior positions.

## Significance
Established Skidmore deference as the baseline for informal agency guidance; contrasted with Chevron deference for formal rules.
