## Rule
Thin capitalization alone does not justify piercing the corporate veil; the plaintiff must show that the corporation was used as a device to perpetrate fraud or injustice, not merely that it was undercapitalized.

## Significance
Reinforced that insufficient capitalization is only one factor; fraudulent use of the corporate form is the key requirement.
