## Rule
The forfeiture-by-wrongdoing exception to the Confrontation Clause applies only when the defendant engaged in the wrongful conduct with the purpose of preventing the witness from testifying; mere killing that happens to silence a witness is insufficient.

## Significance
Narrowed the forfeiture-by-wrongdoing exception; defendant must have intended to silence the witness, not just caused their unavailability.
