## Rule
§ 1983 plaintiffs are bound by prior state court judgments under 28 U.S.C. § 1738; there is no § 1983 exception to claim or issue preclusion.

## Significance
Held that state court adjudications have full preclusive effect in subsequent § 1983 actions in federal court.
