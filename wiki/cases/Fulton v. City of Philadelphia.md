## Rule
A government policy that allows for individualized exemptions but denies one to a religious organization is subject to strict scrutiny under the Free Exercise Clause.

## Significance
Applied Employment Division v. Smith's individualized exemption doctrine to invalidate Philadelphia's refusal to contract with a Catholic foster care agency; declined to overrule Smith.
