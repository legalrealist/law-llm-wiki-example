## Rule
A plaintiff who suffers a procedural due process violation but no actual injury is entitled only to nominal damages; substantial damages require proof of actual harm caused by the constitutional deprivation.

## Significance
Limited § 1983 damages for procedural due process violations to nominal damages absent actual injury.
