## Rule
A defendant can be convicted of RICO conspiracy under 18 U.S.C. § 1962(d) without personally committing or agreeing to commit two predicate acts of racketeering, so long as the defendant agreed to the overall objective of the conspiracy.

## Significance
Broadened RICO conspiracy liability by holding that agreement to the enterprise's corrupt objectives — not personal commission of predicate acts — is the core of § 1962(d), enabling prosecution of peripheral participants.
