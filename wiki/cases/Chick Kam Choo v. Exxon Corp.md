## Rule
The relitigation exception to the Anti-Injunction Act applies only when the federal court has actually adjudicated the issue and preclusion would apply; it does not extend to issues the federal court merely could have decided.

## Significance
Narrowed the relitigation exception to require prior adjudication on the merits, not just that the issue was raised.
