## Rule
28 U.S.C. § 1782 does not extend to private international arbitration panels, which are not "foreign or international tribunals" within the statute's meaning.

## Significance
Narrowed Intel's broad reading of § 1782 by clarifying that only governmental or intergovernmental adjudicative bodies qualify as foreign or international tribunals, foreclosing § 1782 discovery in aid of private arbitrations.
