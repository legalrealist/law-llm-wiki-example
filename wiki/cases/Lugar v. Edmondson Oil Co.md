## Rule
A private party's joint participation with state officials in a deprivation of property constitutes state action under § 1983; two-part test: (1) the deprivation must be caused by the exercise of a state-created right or rule, and (2) the party must be a state actor.

## Significance
Clarified the state action requirement for § 1983 claims involving private parties acting with state officials.
