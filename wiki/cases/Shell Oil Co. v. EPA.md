## Rule
A final rule is invalid if it is not a "logical outgrowth" of the proposed rule — interested parties must have had fair notice that the final rule was a possible outcome.

## Significance
Applied the logical outgrowth doctrine to invalidate a final rule that differed materially from the proposed rule without adequate notice.
