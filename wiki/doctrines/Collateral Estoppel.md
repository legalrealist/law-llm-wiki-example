# Collateral Estoppel

*See* [[Collateral Estoppel (Issue Preclusion)]] for the full doctrine page.

Collateral estoppel (also called issue preclusion) prevents relitigation of an issue of fact or law that was actually litigated, necessarily decided, and essential to a valid final judgment in prior litigation, by a party (or privy) who had a full and fair opportunity to litigate it.
