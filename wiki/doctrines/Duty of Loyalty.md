# Duty of Loyalty

*See* [[Duty of Loyalty (Corporations)]] for the full doctrine page.

The duty of loyalty requires directors and officers to place the corporation's interests above their own. Self-dealing transactions are subject to entire fairness review unless ratified by a disinterested body after full disclosure.
