# Res Judicata

*See* [[Res Judicata (Claim Preclusion)]] for the full doctrine page.

Res judicata (also called claim preclusion) bars relitigation of any claim that was or could have been raised in prior litigation between the same parties (or their privies) that resulted in a final judgment on the merits.
